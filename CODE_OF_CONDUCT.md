# Code of conduct

TailorUp is a small project and wants to stay one that people are glad to
contribute to. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Expected

- Critique code and ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone's first PR was someone's first PR.
- Say what you mean plainly, and accept the same in return.
- Respect a "no" on a feature. It's about scope, not about you.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, including resume content or
  contact details from test data, without their consent.
- Sustained disruption: trolling, derailing threads, or pile-ons.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
GitHub (**@nirmalchatur**) rather than replying in the thread. Reports are kept
confidential. You'll get a response, and nobody will be penalised for a report
made in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on a response proportional to what happened: a
private note, a public request to stop, removal of comments, or a temporary or
permanent ban from the repository. The maintainer is held to the same
standard.
